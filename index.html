Questions

Things people actually ask.

Are these tools really free?
Yes — completely. No signup, no subscription, no hidden charges. The tools are supported by display ads on some pages, but you can use every feature without paying a rupee or a dollar.
How accurate is the AI ticket scanner?
Our scanner uses Google Gemini Vision and achieves 90–95% accuracy on clear ticket photos. Accuracy drops if the ticket is heavily wrinkled, faded, or photographed in poor lighting. We always recommend verifying your winning numbers against the official lottery source before claiming any prize.
Which lotteries do you support?
Right now: US Powerball, US Mega Millions, Kerala State Lottery (all seven weekly draws — Samrudhi, Bhagyathara, Sthree Sakthi, Dhanalekshmi, Karunya Plus, Suvarna Keralam, and Karunya), plus Nagaland Dear Lottery (Morning, Day, Evening, Night draws). More state and country lotteries are being added — let us know which one you want next.
Do you store my ticket photos?
No. Your image is sent to our AI vision service for one-time processing and immediately discarded. We don't keep, sell, or use your data to train models. The only thing we log is anonymous usage statistics (how many scans, which lotteries) so we can improve the service.
Is this affiliated with any official lottery?
No. MintyDeals Tools is an independent, unofficial result-checking service. We are not affiliated with the Government of Kerala, Nagaland State Government, Multi-State Lottery Association (MUSL), or any other lottery operator. Always verify wins with the official lottery source before claiming.
Can I buy lottery tickets here?
No. We do not sell lottery tickets and we don't recommend any online lottery ticket sellers for Indian lotteries (the Government of Kerala and Nagaland do not authorize online ticket sales — beware of fake apps). Purchase tickets only from registered physical agents.
I have a feature request. How do I send it?
Email us at hello@mintydeals.com with your suggestion. We read every email and prioritize based on what most users ask for. Recent community-requested features that we shipped: Nagaland Dear Lottery support, Hindi-language results, and the mobile camera scan flow.
